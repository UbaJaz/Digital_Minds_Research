## Why this file exists

`02_design_audit.md` is the project's preregistration. Its fifteen decision rows (P1–P15) carry an
explicit dated human confirmation ("**YES** — Jaswin, 2026-08-15"). The post-freeze amendments do
not: their status lines read "proposed by Claude Code", "drafted in Claude Code", and/or "awaiting
Jaswin's confirmation". Several of them changed what the study reports, including the primary
reported estimand (A4) and the probe that supplies the report's headline positive result (A8).

That distinction is disclosed in `10_report.md` (Methods §3, Limitations, "Code and Data") and must
not be smoothed over. This file is the working register of what is still outstanding. **It records
what needs confirming; it does not confirm anything.**

Rule for this file and for `02_design_audit.md`: an amendment is marked confirmed only when a human
author writes the confirmation themselves. Nothing here may be filled in on their behalf.

## 2. Other provenance items needing an author decision

1. **Author Contributions wording around VO-E / the fifth persona pair requires author
   confirmation.** `10_report.md` currently reads: "J.C. led the literature grounding, designed the
   hidden-property task and the five persona pairs including both style-equalising scaffolds, and
   owned the pilot feasibility judgment." The count (five) matches the report and the audit. The
   *attribution* is what needs checking: `02_design_audit.md` records `VO-D` as "drafted in Claude
   Code … awaiting Jaswin's screening sign-off" (A1) and `VO-E` as "proposed by Claude Code" (A5),
   under rows P10/P13 which allow Claude-drafted, human-screened clauses. The report's LLM Usage
   Statement already discloses Claude-drafted persona clauses. **No change has been made to the
   contributions sentence**, because rewriting credit is an author decision, not an editorial one.
   The authors should choose one of:
   - leave as is, if J.C. did in fact author or direct all five pairs;
   - or amend to record the drafting route, e.g. "…designed the hidden-property task and the five
     persona pairs, including both style-equalising scaffolds (drafted in Claude Code under rows
     P10/P13 and human-screened)…".

   is outstanding. If the screening did happen, it should be dated in `02_design_audit.md` by the

   deviation from P13.
